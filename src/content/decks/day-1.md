## Coding is very powerful…

…but very difficult to learn.

To write a programming language like **JavaScript** or **Python**, you have to learn to think **just like a computer**: exact, step by step, no mistakes.

<!-- notes:
Most professional programmers study for years. One missing character can break everything.
-->

## How we vibe code

1. **Choose a stack:** the set of technology the AI will use
2. **Prompt:** describe the site you want to the AI
3. **Iterate:** check the result, tell the AI what to improve. Repeat!
4. **Ship it:** publish it to the internet

<!-- notes:
Stack: a marketing website, an online shop, and a tour booking site each need different
tools. You don't have to know them. AI can help you choose!
Step 3 is where you spend most of your time, and it's the real skill.
Say each new word out loud ("stack", "prompt", "iterate", "ship") and have the room repeat it.
-->
