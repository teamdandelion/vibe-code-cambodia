## When you're stuck

1. **Tell the AI exactly what you see.** Copy the error message.
2. **Say what you expected.** "I expected a blue button, I got a red one."
3. **Go back.** "Undo your last change." Git remembers everything.
4. **Start a fresh chat** if the AI seems confused.
